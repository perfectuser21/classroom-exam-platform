# Specialty, theory bank and draft paper

The drive uses an isolated gateway state and synthetic administrator. It creates an occupational standard (specialty and level), creates and edits a theory question, classifies it under a module and knowledge point, lists it by standard and level, and builds a weighted paper retaining the question's ID. It also checks deletion of an unused draft and protection of a question used by a paper. The existing XLSX/Word import, classification and Moodle publication paths remain in use.

Draft papers can contain questions that have not yet been published. Publishing the paper requires a successful Moodle publication for every question; the current Moodle IDs are resolved at publication time. This drive does not publish questions or papers to Moodle or verify an exam.
